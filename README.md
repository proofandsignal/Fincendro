# Fincendro

Open `demo/index.html` in a browser to compare synthetic crypto-backed loan terms. All calculations run locally. Each card identifies the fixture source and date for its fields. Unknown fees or market terms exclude an offer from the cost ranking. The referral simulator requires all three example conditions. Live referrals are disabled.

Fincendro is an independent product. DeFi Credit & Yield Lab is a potential source of verified credit data for future iterations. Real provider terms, permission to use data, market eligibility, referral rights and commercial terms require separate verification.

## Product and commercial plan

Fincendro starts with crypto-backed credit comparison and DeFi collateral risk. The first workflow shows a 12-month cost estimate and collateral shocks of 10%, 20% and 30%. A fall in collateral value can push a position past its liquidation threshold even if the debt stays unchanged.

The proposed business uses no Fincendro lending capital. A third-party lender or protocol supplies the liquidity. Fincendro provides comparison software. Prospective revenue streams are a provider-paid referral fee under written terms and a paid business pilot for the calculator or monitoring software. Partner acceptance, rates, contracts and revenue are unverified. Unsecured consumer loans are a separate possible later phase.

Development can proceed alongside Project Boost in available capacity. Official launch follows the already planned active demos. Independent review, physical Samsung QA and owner approval precede public presentation.
